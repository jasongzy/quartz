## 连接关系

- 因为：because, since, as, for, due to, caused by, on account of, considering, taking sth. into consideration, given that, owing to, be attributed to, be ascribed to, stem from
- 所以：so, therefore, thus, thereby, hence, consequently, accordingly, lead to, incur, as a result (consequence), result in, in consequence, to this end
- 目的：in order to, with the purpose of, be aimed at, be intended to, so as to
- 转折：but, however, instead, while, although, albeit, yet, whereas, nevertheless, nonetheless, in contrast, on the contrary, oppositely, notwithstanding
- 顺序：first(ly), initially, above all, first of all, in the first place, to start with, second(ly), for one thing, on the one hand, on the other hand, ... are twofold, finally, eventually, ultimately
- 并列 / 递进：and, then, after that, afterwards, thereafter, besides, additionally, in addition, what's more, moreover, furthermore
- 解释：specifically, concretely, to be specific, in other words, that is (to say), namely, technically
- 强调：especially, particularly, note that, notably
- 关于：about, as to, as for, in (with) regard to, with respect to, in relation to, in terms of, when it comes to
- 总结：in summary, in conclusion, in general, generally speaking, in short, in brief, overall, on the whole, by and large, to sum up
- 其他连接表达：admittedly, after all, contribute to, to the best of our knowledge
